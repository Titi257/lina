Lina 1.4.0

  • Ringtone for incoming calls: four sounds, adjustable volume, a soft beep during another call, silent in Do Not Disturb.
  • Ready-made account types: FRITZ!Box, Freebox, 1&1, Linphone, Placetel, sipgate, Telekom and Zadarma. Lina fills in the server and transport and tells you where to find your credentials.
  • New optional authentication name, needed by Telekom (e-mail address).
  • After saving an account, the editor shows “Online” or why it failed, with the fix to try.
  • Fixed a crash 30 seconds after editing or deleting an account whose server did not answer.
  • Quick hold then resume: the call now resumes.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Return to answer, Esc to decline, ⇧⌘H to hang up, ⇧⌘M to mute, digits sent during a call, ⌘1 to ⌘6 for the sections.
  • An incoming call shows the window without taking the keyboard from the app you are using.
  • Lines register again as soon as the network changes (Wi-Fi, VPN).
  • Menu bar: why a line failed, and a Call back menu.
  • Clearer error messages, in your language.
