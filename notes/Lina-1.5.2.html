Lina 1.5.2

  • Ringtone: import one of your own audio files as the ringtone (Settings, Sound), and two new built-in sounds, Harp and Vintage telephone. As before, each can be previewed and has its own volume, separate from the call volume.
  • Compact window: the minimum height goes down to 560 points, and the dial pad no longer leaves empty space above the number and below the call button.
