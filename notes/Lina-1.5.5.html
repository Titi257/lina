What's new in Lina 1.5.5

  • Call links: Settings now shows which app opens phone-number links on your Mac, and how to make Lina your choice when macOS does not let Lina do it for you.
  • Contacts in your order: the list follows the sort order of the Contacts app, with the sorting name in bold, as in Apple's list.
  • Clearer language setting: “System language (English)” shows which language Lina follows.
