What's new in Lina 1.7.0

  • Light mode: Lina now comes in light as well as dark. By default it follows your Mac's appearance; in Settings, General, Appearance, you can keep it light or dark at all times.
  • Your own shortcut: the global shortcut that answers or hangs up from any app is no longer fixed. In Settings, General, click the Shortcut field and type the combination you prefer; “Default” brings back ⌃⌥Space.
  • A site of its own: Lina has moved to lina-softphone.com. The links to buy Lina Pro and to the licence agreement now lead there.
