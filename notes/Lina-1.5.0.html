Lina 1.5.0

  • Three-way conference (Lina Pro): join your two calls from the strip of the call on hold, then split them again or end the conference in one click. Mute covers both parties.
  • Compact window: the window now shrinks down to the size of the dial pad; the sidebar becomes a row of icons.
  • Mac Contacts (Lina Pro): your contacts' photos during calls and in the list, and your lists from the Contacts app.
  • Recordings folder (Lina Pro): choose it in Settings, General. A notification names each new recording and shows it in the Finder.
  • Statistics and Settings adapt to a narrow window.
