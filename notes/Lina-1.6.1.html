What's new in Lina 1.6.1

  • Sidebar fixed: in 1.6.0 a click on Keypad, History and the other sections of the sidebar no longer did anything. They work again. Sorry for the trouble.
  • Your hours: in Settings, General, Availability, tell Lina when you take calls. Outside those hours it switches to “Do Not Disturb” by itself: calls go to your voicemail and stay in the history as missed calls.
  • Call notes: write down what was said or promised on any call of the history (the “…” button). The note comes back on screen the next time that person calls, and in the contact's card.
  • Callbacks: mark a call “Call Back”, with a reminder in an hour, tomorrow morning or whenever you choose. Calls to return stay at the top of the history until you have spoken to the person.
  • Part of Lina Pro: these three features are included in your licence, and in the 14-day trial.
  • One more column: the CSV export of the history now carries your notes.
