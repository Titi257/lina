Lina 1.3.0

  • Lina is now available in German. Pick it in Settings, General, or let Lina follow your Mac's language.
  • Incoming calls now reach Lina reliably behind a router or a VPN: Lina keeps the connection to your provider open between registrations.
  • Hanging up a call yourself before it is answered no longer shows « Call failed ».
  • Settings, Network: the REGISTER expiry field no longer shows its value twice.
