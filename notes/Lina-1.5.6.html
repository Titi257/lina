What's new in Lina 1.5.6

  • Call links that work: on recent versions of macOS, “Use Lina” in Settings now really makes Lina the app that calls when you click a phone number, in your browser, a CRM or Contacts.
  • Contacts as in the Contacts app: the same order as Apple's list, and each number shows its label (mobile, work, your own labels).
  • A clearer history: calls are grouped by period (today, yesterday, this week…), show which number was used, and an (i) button opens the contact's card.
  • A steadier dial pad: keys and digits keep their size in the smallest window.
  • Grey circles, if you prefer: an option in Settings, General, for contacts without a photo.
  • Readable update notes: they are now properly formatted in every language.
